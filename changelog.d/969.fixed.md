`strip_search_qualifiers` no longer leaves a stray `(` or `[` when the input contained an unbalanced wrapper shape such as `(created:>2025-03-20` (opener with no closer). The lone opener is now removed after the fixpoint loop, provided it is fully isolated by whitespace — legitimate unmatched parens that are part of real topic text (e.g. `ai (foo bar`) are preserved unchanged.
